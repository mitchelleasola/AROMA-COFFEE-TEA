WALK IN AND SMELL THE COFFEE!

Aroma Cofffe and Tea House started as a local Kenyan kibanda where Mai Shai, the founder, would prepare and serve coffee for fifteen years before expanding to become the beloved establishment it is today. We are dedicated to providing the highest quality of coffee and tea in a warm and welcoming environment.

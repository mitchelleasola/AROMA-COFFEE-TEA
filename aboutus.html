Aroma was established in 1972 by Mai Shai, a passionate coffee enthusiast who wanted to share the rich flavors of Kenyan coffee with the world.


The mission of Aroma Coffee & Tea House is to provide the highest quality coffee and tea in a warm and welcoming environment.
Our vision is to be the premier destination for coffee and tea lovers, offering an exceptional experience that combines quality, warmth, and hospitality.
